Contact us

Email keyacesoftware@gmail.com. We read every message within 24 hours. Please don't include personal details like your address, school or phone number.

Report a player

  1. During or after a game, tap 🚩 Report under the chat.
  2. Say what happened (rude chat, cheating, a bad name…).
  3. Your email app opens with the report filled in, including the player's recent messages. Tap Send.

Reports are private: they go only to us and are never posted anywhere.

Block a player

Tap 🔇 Block in a game. You won't see their messages, and they can't join your invites or be matched with you again. Blocked players are listed on the Online Play page, where you can unblock them.

For parents

  • Elo Ladder has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Progress is saved only on the device.
  • When setting up, players enter their age. Quick match only pairs players in the same age group: 12 and under, 13–17, or 18 and over.
  • Ages can't be verified, so players 12 and under can only send ready-made messages ("Good luck!", "Nice move!", "Good game!"…) to people they don't know. Typed messages to or from them are blocked.
  • Players 13–17 also start with ready-made messages only and can choose to turn on typed chat (with a swear-word filter) for a game.
  • Typed chat is always available when playing a friend you invited with a link.
  • Talk with your child about never sharing their real name, where they live, their school or other apps, and about using Report and Block.

Common questions

How do I change my name? Tap your name at the top, then Change name. You can change it once, then once every 180 days.

My rating seems wrong. Each mode has its own rating. Tap ELO at the top to see them all, or take the Elo Test.

Quick match can't find anyone. Quick match pairs you with someone in your age group who picked the same time control at the same moment. Try a more popular one, like Blitz 3 + 2, or invite a friend with a link.
