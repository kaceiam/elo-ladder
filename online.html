Play on your phone

  1. Point your phone's camera at the code and tap the link that pops up.
  2. Install it like an app:
    iPhone (Safari): tap Share ⎙ → Add to Home Screen.
    Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open it from your home screen and hit a quick-play button — you'll be matched with anyone else playing.
