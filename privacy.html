Privacy Policy

Elo Ladder · last updated October 10, 2026

Elo Ladder is a free chess app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share data with anyone.

What stays on your device

  • Your player name, ratings, game history, training progress, trophies, board style and settings.
  • This is saved only in the app's storage on your phone or in your browser. Deleting the app (or clearing your browser data) deletes it.
  • Games against the bots run entirely on your device using the Stockfish chess engine. Nothing about those games is sent anywhere.

Playing a friend

  • When you invite a friend, your device and theirs connect directly to each other. To set up that connection, a free public connection service (PeerJS) briefly sees a random invite code and your network address — the same thing any website you visit sees.
  • Moves, chat messages and your player name and rating are sent only to the friend you're playing.

Online lobby (only if the owner's server is running)

  • If you use the optional online lobby, the game server stores your player name, rating, game history, friends list, chat messages and the network address you connected from, so the game can work and stay fair (for example, to stop cheating and abuse).
  • That information stays on the server's computer and is only visible to the game's administrator.

Chat safety: filter, report and block

  • Chat messages are filtered: swear words and slurs are replaced with stars before anyone sees them.
  • Tap 🚩 Report in any game to report a player. In the online lobby the report goes straight to the game's administrator, with the player's recent chat as evidence. In Play a Friend (which has no server) the report opens your email app with a private, pre-filled message to our support address. Reports are never posted publicly.
  • Tap 🔇 Block to block a player: you won't see their messages, and they can't join your invites, challenge you, friend you or be matched with you. You can unblock them any time.
  • Reports are reviewed and acted on (warnings, name changes, restrictions or bans) within 24 hours.

Children

Elo Ladder is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Player names are checked to block inappropriate words.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions or requests? Open an issue at github.com/kaceiam/elo-ladder/issues.
